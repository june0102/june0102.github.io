M.S. student, Electrical Engineering, Korea University

I’m a master’s student at Korea University in Seoul. I’m interested in how agents can learn models of the world and use them to plan and act: world models for planning and model-based reinforcement learning, vision-language-action models and generative robot policies, and offline-to-online RL for adapting policies.

Before that, I studied Linguistics (second major in Food & Resource Economics) at Korea University, and was a research intern at the System 2 Lab, where I worked on prompt optimization with Generative Flow Networks.
